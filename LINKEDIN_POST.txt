LINKEDIN POST — INVEXOM CYBERSECURITY INTERNSHIP

I completed my Cybersecurity Internship task under the mentorship of Invexom.

Task: Perform a Basic Security Awareness Assessment

For this project, I created a practical cybersecurity awareness assessment for a fictional small organization, NovaBridge Solutions.

The assessment identifies 12 common employee-facing security risks, including:
• Weak/reused passwords
• Phishing and credential theft
• Unsafe downloads
• Unsecured devices
• Social engineering
• Improper data sharing
• Outdated software
• Untrusted links
• Unsafe public Wi-Fi
• Unauthorized USB/removable media
• Missing MFA
• Excessive access privileges

For every risk, I documented the risk description, potential impact, example scenario, and recommended prevention.

I also created an employee cybersecurity checklist focused on practical daily security habits such as MFA, secure password practices, device protection, safe data sharing, phishing awareness, and incident reporting.
